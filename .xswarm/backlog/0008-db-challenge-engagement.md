## Problem
Chad, 2026-09-06: for the Dawn-Breakers Challenge, a custom WhatsApp group and
a series of planned announcements about resources and translations leading up
to the December programme. **"We should surprise everyone each week."**

## The design constraint that matters
Weekly surprise cannot be improvised — a schedule of surprises is a
contradiction only if the surprises are not prepared in advance. The plan is a
calendar of prepared reveals, so that the week it is due, the material already
exists.

Programme runs 25 December – 3 January. Counting back, that is roughly fifteen
weeks of announcements, which means fifteen prepared items.

## Note
The corpus work in siftersearch and CTAI is a natural source: a newly aligned
translation, a newly surfaced account, a person profile. The archive can supply
the surprises if they are queued far enough ahead.
