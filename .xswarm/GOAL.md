# Goal

_Confirmed by Chad in interview, 2026-09-05._

## What this is for

The website of the **Desert Rose Bahá’í Institute** — a real organisation Chad
helps run. Not a project in the portfolio sense: an organisation with people,
programmes and obligations, whose site serves it.

## What success looks like

People who want to attend, enrol or get in touch can do so without friction,
and the organisation is not held up waiting on the website.

At ~40 pageloads a day, steady, it is the third-busiest property on the account
and the traffic looks like real people rather than crawlers.

## Open — Chad to settle

**What should the site cause?** Enrolment, contact, donation, event attendance?
Until that is named, there is no way to say whether the site is working. This
is the one thing blocking a real KPI.

## Explicitly not the goal

Growth for its own sake. This serves an existing organisation and its actual
audience.
